The Second Layer: Zampano's story

This layer is the one made by Zampanno, the old blind man who writes about the events of the House as he discovered them in the Navidson records. He was an old man who lived in the appartment where Johnny now lives. He had someone help him to write as much as possible about the House and he also had digressions during sometimes dozens of pages on very diverse subjects

Fun fact: the printed version of House of not tree leaves, meant to be a representation of Zampano's work and interpretation of the Navidson Records, is bigger on the inside than it is on the outside. How can a book be bigger on the inside? Simply make the front cover too small to cover the first page of the book.
