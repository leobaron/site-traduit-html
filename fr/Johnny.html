The First Layer of the Book: Johnny's Discovery

Johnny Truand

Johnny Truand is the first character we encounter in this story, he works in a tattoo shop in L.A. and lives a careless life. His introduction to the House happens through a manuscript he found in his new appartment. The appartment was previously occupied by Mr Zampano, a blind old man who wrote about the story of the House.

At first, Johnny isn' that interested in the story but he quickly becomes obsessed with Zampano's manuscripts and tries to put the pages in order by digging through piles a written notes the old man left in the appartment. Johnny will try to find the House to see if Zampano's story is real or just a product of his imagination. His search for the truth will destroy him physically and mentally to the point where he isn't able to keep track of time anymore.
