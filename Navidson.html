The third layer: The Navidson family

This part of the book tells us the story of the Navidson familiy through a collection of tapes made by the father to document their exploration of the House.

. These tapes are known as "The Navidson Records".

the Navidson family members:

Will Navidson

Will Navidson is the main subject of Zampano's book. He was a renown photojournalist who faced severe backlash when he published a troubling picture of a little girl on the brink of death followed by a vulture waiting behind her. When he moves to the House on Ash Tree Lane, he sets up cameras to document the new life of his family. Eventually the family learns that the House is changing shape. Navidson first decides to explore the House but after a tragic series of events he decides it's best to move out. Still he can't stay away for the House and chooses to go back in for a final expolration.

Tom

Tom is Will's brother. He was called by Will to help him out with the exploration House. With his help, the confirmed that the House is indeed bigger on the inside than it is on the outside

Karen

Karen is Will's wife, she was hoping to live a calmer life by moving in the House on Ash Tree Lane. She is the first one to be scared when the unusual properties of the House are discovered. She often begs Will and the other people exploring the corridors of the House to just stop and leave it be as she believes it is stupid and dangerous to try and understand such a place.

The Children

The Navidson children are called Chad and Daisy. They are the first ones to discover the hidden corridors of the House. One of them appeared in their room and they went inside to play, that's when their parents, hearing the laughters coming from inside the walls start to look for them and see them in the darkened mysterious Hallway.

The Start Of The Exploration

After the incident with the children, Navidson asks his brother, a structural engineer, for help to measure the House and they realize that it is slightly bigger inside than it is outside, which shouldn't be possible. Later, they decide to go explore the corridor and turn the exploration into a multiple day expedition helped by professionals: a mountain climber, a hunter and a former military. Things get heated during the expolration, one of them ends up injured and another kills himself out of desperation. Navidson decides that he will take one last journey in the House and goes back through the corridor by bike, doing about 500km per day until he can't go any further and decides to turn around and leave.
